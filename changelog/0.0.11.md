# Firstworks 0.0.11

## Added

- Bone and Flint Knives for fibre gathering, hide scraping, and low-yield renewable fleece harvesting.
- Nine-slot, automation-capable Woven Basket.
- Physical charcoal mounds made from connected world logs and configurable sealant blocks; finished charcoal is revealed when the mound is opened.
- Animated in-world Mortar & Pestle workstation with data-driven grinding recipes, KubeJS schema support, and JEI integration.
- Raw Ochre, Ground Ochre, Charcoal Powder, and Copper Fasteners.
- Public `#firstworks:charcoal_igniters` tag and Mortar KubeJS start/completion events.
- Data-driven animal material tags:
  - `#firstworks:drops_bones`
  - `#firstworks:no_bone_drops`
  - `#firstworks:leather_drops_as_raw_hide`
  - `#firstworks:no_raw_hide_drops`

## Changed

- Primitive precision work now requires knives instead of accepting combat swords.
- Animal bone drops are now owned by Firstworks and configurable independently.
- Raw-hide replacement is restricted by entity-type tags rather than applying broadly to every `Animal` instance.
- Default bone drops now cover vertebrate animals and exclude squid, glow squid, bees, allays, and undead horses.
