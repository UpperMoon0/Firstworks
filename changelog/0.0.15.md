# Firstworks 0.0.15

## Manual workstation rework

- Stone Anvils now support ordered flatten, draw, and bend actions selected by the struck region. Copper forging requires heat; use the restored Kiln to heat the workpiece, then carry it to the anvil. The kiln only heats items and never advances actions or smelts them.
- Workpieces carry heat and completed actions between stations and through saves. Heat fades in storage and inventory. Item overlays and anvil workpiece models glow with heat; the kiln and anvil emit fading block light.
- Added solid deformable anvil workpieces, optional custom stage models, hovered-region outlines, and action hints. Recipes without visual metadata retain their item model; existing simple smashing recipes remain supported.
- Stone Anvils have a small light Flatten pad, wider dark Draw strips around the top, and horn-only Bend. Hammer hits, outlines, and Jade's targeted action share bounded surfaces in every facing; side faces and lower ledges cannot select a forging action.
- Looms load a stack in one action. Hold the visible shuttle and guide it across to place a loose row; packing commits each row. Partial crossings survive release/reload, clicks cannot bypass strokes, and optional client input assistance uses the same timing. Basic cloth remains four passes. JEI shows ingredients, output, and required shuttle crossings.
- Mortars now distinguish center crushing from held rim grinding. Releasing use pauses grinding, and saved work resumes without autonomous completion. Built-in charcoal, bone, ochre, and grog recipes use explicit stages.
- Workstation processing remains server-authoritative, with per-tick guards against duplicate progress and saved partial work across reloads.

## Correctness and integrations

- Crucible glow, molten contents, and heat effects now require an active paid batch with airflow; retained progress alone no longer makes the furnace hot.
- Barrel JEI displays now apply each recipe's configured `input_count` to every item/tag alternative instead of showing tag inputs as a single item.
- Added 0.0.14 save migration for partial Stone Anvil and active Mortar work so upgrades preserve progress instead of misreading or discarding it.
- Require Patchouli with a craftable Firstworks Field Guide covering all features, manual controls, heat, pausing, automation boundaries, recipes, and settings.
- Updated in-world hints, Jade status, JEI recipes, and item tooltips for the changed stations, including custom recipe requirements and cancellation feedback.
- Overlapping loom and mortar recipes select the largest affordable input count, then recipe id. Hints and rendering use that same selection; before enough input is loaded, hints show the smallest matching requirement. Mortars permit loading larger matching batches before work begins.
- Preserved optional recipe metadata, network synchronization, and KubeJS lifecycle hooks. Unowned scripted mortar starts no longer trigger autonomous grinding.
- Added regression coverage for action ordering, heat, facing, patterns, partial saves, manual pauses, custom recipes, overlapping recipes, script lifecycle events, duplicate work, JEI input stack counts, and legacy-NBT migration.

## Packmakers

- See `docs/PACKMAKERS.md` for anvil `forge`, loom `weaving`, and mortar `processing` metadata and fallback behavior.
- Targets NeoForge 1.21.1. Brick-mold and pottery interaction redesigns are outside this release.

- Holding the loom shuttle now captures Use input, including through frame gaps and brief packet delays, to avoid activating blocks behind it. Finished cloth is displayed and collected as a separate roll below the working fabric, so subsequent batches retain visible progress.
- Expired workpiece heat is normalized during stack comparisons, allowing cooled items in storage and automated transfers to merge while preserving forging progress and keeping hot stacks distinct.

- Kilns and Crucible Furnaces require explicit ignition with a Fire Starter, Flint and Steel, Fire Charge, or a compatible modded igniter. Fuel loading and Bellows air never light a cold station. Added `#firstworks:workstation_igniters` alongside NeoForge fire-starting ability support, ignition hints, and burnout/relighting coverage.

- Correct loom shuttle/beater texture orientation to preserve pixel proportions. Item heat tooltips now show a single temperature/status/countdown line, with per-item server maximum temperatures and a client Celsius/Fahrenheit/Kelvin display setting.

- Require Patchouli on client and server, declare its required release relation, and expand the Field Guide to eight categories covering all features and recipes. Add a custom transparent 64×64 book sprite.
- Correct Jade loom direction arrows to match the player view from the working side.
- Give Plaster Blocks a custom 16×16 lime-plaster texture instead of vanilla Calcite.
- Show the Firstworks Field Guide in the Firstworks creative tab.
- Explain fuel, ignition, workable heat, retrieval, and anvil transfer on the kiln JEI page, with an igniter slot and wrapped instructions.

- Unify workstation and item heat around Celsius temperatures. Add recipe temperature requirements, temporary Bellows ceiling boosts with hunger cost, and current/maximum temperature in Jade.
- Burn native furnace fuels continuously across completed recipes, automatically consuming the next fuel item at burnout. Limit kiln heating input to one item while keeping fuel stackable.
- Move Copper Fasteners to heated Stone Anvil forging and update JEI, the Field Guide, and packmaker documentation.

- Remove persistent center-screen hints for kiln, anvil, mortar, and loom; Jade retains workstation guidance. Keep spatial anvil outlines.
- Fix heated inventory and hotbar icons by using a GUI-specific glow layer without the world rendering depth offset.

- Simplify Crucible Furnace JEI: machine-only sidebar, no duplicate station slot, vanilla flame between fuel/input, reusable catalyst NC overlay with tooltip, and seconds instead of ticks.
- Slow default thermal pacing: kiln heats over 30 seconds, copper forging stays workable about 100 seconds, and Bellows hold 30 seconds before a 15-second decay. Crucibles warm by 1°C/tick and cool by 0.25°C/tick while idle.

- Add the missing Crucible Furnace igniter slot and label Fuel, Bellows, and Igniter requirements. Both heated-station JEI pages list all supported igniters, including tagged and modded fire-starting items.
