# Firstworks 0.0.12

## Added

- Quern (`firstworks:quern`): a bulk grinding workstation crafted with stone, wooden slabs, and Copper Fasteners.
- Dynamic 3D runner model with central feed eye and vertical wooden crank peg, rendered with smooth client rotation interpolation.
- Unified `work` schema for data-driven `firstworks:quern_grinding` recipes with manual empty-hand cranking (+5 work/crank) and external continuous drive support.
- Public `QuernDriveable` interface for clean integration with animal power (Create Horse Power) and external kinetic systems.
- Flour (`firstworks:flour`) and common `#c:flours`/`#c:flours/wheat` tags.
- Replaced raw wheat with milled flour (`#c:flours/wheat`) in vanilla Bread, Cake, and Cookie recipes.
- JEI category, Jade status, NeoForge item capabilities (top input, bottom output), and KubeJS `quernGrindingStarting`/`quernGrindingCompleted` lifecycle events.

## Changed

- Progression establishes that wheat is an agricultural crop while flour is a food ingredient: grain must be milled in the Quern before crafting prepared food.
- Distinguishes precision Mortar & Pestle grinding (Stone Age), bulk manual/animal Quern milling (Early Copper Age), and industrial Create Millstone processing.
