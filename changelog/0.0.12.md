# Firstworks 0.0.12

## Added

- Quern (`firstworks:quern`): a hand-operated bulk grinding workstation crafted with stone, wooden slabs, and Copper Fasteners.
- Dynamic 3D runner model with central feed eye and vertical wooden crank peg, rendered with smooth client rotation interpolation.
- Unified `work` schema for data-driven `firstworks:quern_grinding` recipes with configurable manual empty-hand cranking (`quernManualWorkPerCrank`, default 5).
- The Quern is intentionally player-operated, establishing a manual bulk-processing tier before mechanized milling while still supporting automated item insertion and output extraction.
- Flour (`firstworks:flour`) in `#c:flours`/`#c:flours/wheat`, plus Wheat Dough (`firstworks:dough`) with a custom 64x64 transparent sprite in `#c:doughs`/`#c:doughs/wheat`.
- Water crafting recipes for Wheat Dough from buckets, clay buckets, and water bottles.
- Campfire, smoker, and furnace cooking recipes turning Wheat Dough into Bread.
- Updated vanilla recipes for Bread and Cookies to require Wheat Dough, and Cake to require Flour.
- JEI category, Jade status, NeoForge item capabilities (top input, bottom output), and KubeJS `quernGrindingStarting`/`quernGrindingCompleted` lifecycle events.

## Changed

- Progression establishes that wheat is an agricultural crop: grain must be milled in the Quern into Flour and mixed into Dough for prepared baked food.
- Distinguishes precision Mortar & Pestle grinding (Stone Age), manual bulk Quern milling (Early Copper Age), and later mechanized mill processing.
