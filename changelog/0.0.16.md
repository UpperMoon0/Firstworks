# Firstworks 0.0.16

- Primitive knife harvesting replaces ordinary source-block loot with one Raw Ochre and costs one durability. Other tools give no bonus ochre; Silk Touch preserves ordinary harvesting. No world generation is added.
- Craft one Ground Ochre into one vanilla Red Dye. Mortar yields two pigment per raw ochre; the Quern processes four raw ochre into eight pigment in 60 work (12 default cranks).
- Open barrels accept NeoForge fluid-container capabilities, including BucketLib Wooden and Ceramic Buckets and compatible modded fluids. Container restrictions and returned items are respected. Manual transfers fill input and drain output first from every face; automation faces and sealed-lid locks remain.
- Firstworks clay buckets remain water/tannin-only and plain water bottles add 250 mB. Removed obsolete rawOchreGatherChance configuration.

- Creative barrel extraction returns a filled container instead of losing fluid. Jade exposes separate input/output fluid names and amounts; rejected transfers give throttled recovery hints.
- Raw Ochre acquisition is searchable in JEI. Guides distinguish default sources/recipes from pack overrides, include exact tag paths and examples, and keep current player mechanics integrated.
