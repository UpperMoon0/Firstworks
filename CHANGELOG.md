
## 0.0.17

- Hot crucible castings preserve actual station temperature through storage and extraction.
- Water and filled water cauldrons cool hot items faster; hold use to quench held workpieces.
- Ambient cooling and hot-handling protection are enabled by default and independently configurable.
- Added copper-independent Primitive Tongs, heat-protection equipment tags, and contextual guidance.
- Added deterministic datapack/KubeJS cooling treatments, persistent markers, and optional reheating restrictions.
- Tagged modded shears produce Firstworks fleece without duplicate shearing.
